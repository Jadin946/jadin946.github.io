# Computer Science Research

I am an undergraduate Computer Science student at Smith College interested in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and trustworthy computational systems. My work explores how subtle contextual cues influence language-model behavior, as well as questions of accountability and governance in AI.

[Explore my research](#research) · [Contact](#contact)

## Research

### Implicit drift in large language models

An independent research project investigating whether subtle, bias-inducing contextual cues can shift model outputs while they remain compliant with explicit instructions. I developed controlled prompting conditions and an experimental framework to measure differences in model behavior. Pilot experiments are complete, and further work will examine whether implicit drift predicts later explicit instruction violations.

**Topics:** LLM evaluation, AI accountability, experimental design

### Satellite fleet drag detector

An independent computational project exploring the detection of satellite drag-related behavior. More technical details and code will be added as the project documentation is prepared.

**Topics:** computational modeling, anomaly detection

### AI and data as commons

As a Wurtele Futures Fellow at Smith College, I study how AI and data can be understood and governed as shared resources, drawing on interdisciplinary research and expert interviews.

**Topics:** data governance, digital commons, AI ethics
